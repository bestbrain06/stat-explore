01 THE IDEA

Statistics becomes clearer when students can see it.

Measures of Dispersion help us describe how data values are distributed around a dataset. Concepts such as range, quartiles, interquartile range, variance and standard deviation provide important information about the spread of data.

StatExplore was created to give students a more active way of learning these ideas. Instead of only reading formulas, learners can enter data, observe changes, calculate measures and interpret what those results mean.

03 — LEARNING MODEL

Explore. Calculate. Interpret.

StatExplore follows a simple learning cycle that encourages students to move from observing data to making mathematical conclusions.

01

Explore

Enter or examine datasets and observe how the values are distributed.

02

Calculate

Determine the range, quartiles, IQR, variance and standard deviation.

03

Interpret

Connect the numerical results to what they reveal about the spread of the dataset.

04 — WHAT WE EXPLORE

Five ways to understand variation.

The laboratory focuses on the major measures students need to understand how much data values differ from one another.

R

Range

Difference between the largest and smallest values.

Q

Quartiles

Values that divide ordered data into four parts.

IQR

Interquartile Range

The spread of the middle fifty percent of the data.

σ²

Variance

A measure of how far values vary from the mean.

σ

Standard Deviation

A measure of the typical spread of values.
